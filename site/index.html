The full picture.

BanglaWhistle leads Whisper-tiny on the held-out OpenSLR evaluation. External results are shown alongside it—where the baseline currently leads.

✦ Best result · in-domain

Lower character error on held-out Bangla speech.

On 100 held-out clips from OpenSLR 53, BanglaWhistle’s character error rate is lower than the Whisper-tiny baseline. This is a same-corpus evaluation—not evidence of a broad or universal lead.

“Best result” is limited to OpenSLR zip f, held out from training but drawn from the same corpus. FLEURS and Common Voice are external test sets and Whisper-tiny leads on both. RTF reflects the recorded evaluation environment, not hosted-service latency.

Know the limits

BanglaWhistle is a 15.97M-parameter research model, not a general-purpose recognizer. Its cross-corpus results are substantially weaker; check important output before using it.
